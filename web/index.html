    Connect your computer

    A website can't look at your ports by itself. A tiny helper (one file, no install) runs on your computer, reads the ports, and talks only to this page.

    1. Install Node.js 18 or newer.
    2. Download and start the helper in a terminal:
    3. It prints a link. Open it, or paste the token here:

    Chrome may ask to let this site reach devices on your local network: choose Allow. Safari blocks this, so on Safari open the http://127.0.0.1:7777 page the helper prints instead.

    Open a port

    Parks a beacon on a free port: a tiny listener that answers "hello". Handy for testing firewalls or holding a port for later.
